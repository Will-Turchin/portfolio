CapSure
smart pill management for dementia care.

A prototype designed to help semi-autonomous dementia patients follow their medication routines while giving caregivers timely, remote confirmation.

Jan 2025 – May 2025Team of fiveProject contributor: Will Turchin

The challenge

Patients with dementia may forget daily pill doses, placing a substantial burden on caregivers. Our team built a novel dispenser that stores dosage-based capsules, issues scheduled alerts, and records a short confirmation video when a dose is taken.

At the programmed time, the patient presses a button to dispense a dose-ball. A Raspberry Pi controls the system and activates a camera; the resulting video is uploaded to the cloud for caregiver review.

Design question How might we reduce the burden on caregivers of semi-autonomous dementia patients by improving pill-regimen adherence?
Scheduled dispensingPre-programmed intervals and an audible reminder prompt the next dose.
Caregiver verificationA camera records the pill-taking event and uploads it for remote supervision.
Embedded prototypeRaspberry Pi 4, camera, buzzer, servo motor, and a capsule-distribution mechanism.
